03  Process

Nothing gets filmed until you’ve read it.

The expensive mistakes in advertising are made before the camera rolls. So the words come first, you sign them off, and only then do we make pictures.

  1. Step 01

    Brief

    You tell us what you sell, who buys it and what you want them to do. A few lines is enough to start.

  2. Step 02

    Script

    We write the film as a story, with the narration in full. You approve it, or we rewrite it.

  3. Step 03

    Storyboard

    Every shot as a still frame: the cast, the locations, your product. You see the film before it exists.

  4. Step 04

    Film

    We shoot the scenes, record the narration and compose the score.

  5. Step 05

    Cut and deliver

    Edited to the music, captioned and exported in every format you need. Notes are turned round quickly.

Principle / 1

Story before spectacle.

A beginning, a turn and a payoff. Viewers stay for what happens next, so every film is written as one continuous story rather than a list of features.

Principle / 2

Your product, exactly.

The box on the counter is the box you sell. The logo on the van is your logo. We check every frame against the real thing, because your customers will.

Principle / 3

Made the modern way.

We film with generative production tools and finish like any other studio: a proper edit, a mix and a grade. You get film-grade pictures without the cost of a crew.

04  Questions

Before you ask.

How are the films made?

With generative film tools, directed and edited by us. We write the script, design the cast and locations, generate each scene, then cut, score and grade the result. There is no location shoot to schedule, which is why we can work quickly and for less than a traditional production.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the business and who you sell to, your logo, and photos of anything that must appear on screen exactly as it is, such as a product, a vehicle or an app. A link to your website helps too.

How long does it take?

It depends on the length of the film and how quickly the script is approved. You get a timeline with the quote, before any work starts.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted up front and the quote is fixed before we begin. Tell us what you need and we will come back with a price and a timeline.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. The script and the storyboard are both approval points, so most changes happen before filming. Notes on the finished cut are part of the job, and small ones are turned round fast.

Which formats do I get?

A master file plus the versions you need for where the film will run: widescreen for web and TV screens, vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, and square or 4:5 for feeds. Captions can be burned in or supplied separately.
